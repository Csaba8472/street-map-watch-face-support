Street Map Watch Face Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy covers Street Map Watch Face for Wear OS, package com.hcsaba.mapwatchface.

Location and map requests

If you grant location access, the app uses your watch’s location to draw a street map around you. If you grant background location access, the map can update while the app is closed. The app relies on Android and Google Play services to obtain location.

The watch requests nearby map tiles directly from OpenFreeMap over HTTPS. Tile identifiers indicate the area of the map being requested, and OpenFreeMap receives the IP address and app user agent associated with the request. The app does not send raw GPS coordinates in those requests or send your location to a developer-operated server. See OpenFreeMap and its privacy policy for information about its service.

Storage, retention, and deletion

The app keeps the latest location, downloaded map tiles and rendered map, settings, and refresh diagnostics in Android app storage. This enables offline viewing and reduces repeated downloads. The latest location and map are replaced when newer data is saved. Cached tiles are evicted as the cache fills. Android may back up app data according to your device settings. You can revoke location access in your watch’s app permissions and clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove its on-device data.

Accounts and purchases

The watch app has no account sign-in, ads, or developer analytics. Google Play handles the purchase under Google’s privacy policy. The watch app does not receive your payment card details.

Contact

For help or privacy questions, use the developer contact in the Google Play listing.
